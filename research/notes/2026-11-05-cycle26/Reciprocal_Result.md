# Reciprocal return advantage survives a harmless bridge

Decision: a crossed prefix-by-return experiment identifies the effect of substituting one initial visit for another on the specified fixed streams, when forked from identical initial state with paired downstream inputs, randomness, counters, and complete resets. It cannot on its own identify recovery of a function lost because of the intervening task. Strictly better performance in both reciprocal cells and against every fresh learning-rate rescaling is compatible with zero bridge-induced deficit. This is a constructed identifiability result, with zero training and no observations of new empirical labels.

**Estimands.** Let R[h,b,r] be post-update MSE, with pre-update MSE also reported on the exact same evaluation examples, separate from adaptation examples. For b fixed define G_A=R[C,b,A]-R[A,b,A] and G_C=R[A,b,C]-R[C,b,C]. Their sum is a two-by-two interaction contrast and cancels additive prefix and return main effects. Its positivity alone permits harm on one return. Require both margins separately. Across independent stream replicates pair within the same draw of each return and bridge, counterbalance orders or fork independent copies, and avoid any serial carryover between cells. For a statistical population claim, define the draw distribution and replicate over histories, not individual rows from one shared optimizer trajectory. A causal prefix intervention includes every consequence of that earlier visit, including its gradient statistics. Task identity unobserved by the learner cannot be separated from differences in gradient history by that intervention alone.

**Interpretation and design.** The return comparison has a measurable, narrower endpoint: domain-specific readaptation benefit on independent return examples for these prefixes and common downstream schedule. To attribute it to the intervening task, introduce a predeclared neutral bridge with matched counts, duration, no information from return labels and comparable algorithmic budget. Report G_i(active)-G_i(neutral). These are effect modifications, not by themselves evidence of restoration. To measure recovery additionally define a common deficit probe and predeclared meaningful margins, establish that the active bridge causes a deficit versus the neutral bridge, and measure how much of that gap closes after equal return resources. Resetting complete deterministic weights forces identical unadapted outputs; an optimizer-mediated deficit would concern performance after a common standardized probe update. Measure any diagnostic probe on state copies so it does not train the subsequent arm. The proposed settings and controls need empirical justification before a study can be registered. With an unqualified stream and no predicted deficit, keep recovery closed as a paper claim.
