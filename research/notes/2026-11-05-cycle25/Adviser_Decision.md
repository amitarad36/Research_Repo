Close the constructed optimizer-recovery result as a stand-alone paper candidate.** The feasible thesis contribution is a reproducible attribution of changes in IT3 regression updates, paired with direct scoring of prediction error and an explicit identifiability limit. There is a measurable difference in the first trained study but no positive accuracy result. The optimizer control explains why even an update direction outside the fresh learning-rate ray can be caused by the intervening batch. No first-paper benefit or general novelty has been established.

This was a two-paper audit, not a systematic novelty search. The generic benefit or harm of carrying optimizer state is already an empirical question addressed in supervised continual learning. Periodic resets in recurring unlabeled TTA are also established. Our narrower contrast asks if a previous visit yields a *domain-specific accuracy benefit* after restoring identical weights and matching intervening observations and step counts. Neither inspected comparison reports that joint contrast. Cycles 23-24 establish a mechanistic effect only. Reusing the frozen Boston results as an independent confirmation would reuse evidence already seen. The research-folder checkpoint/split inventory and cycle-16/18 provenance do not identify a second qualified checkpoint plus a disjoint recurring stream for this graph. Public recurring-image benchmarks exist; a graph-compatible regressor or classifier, source supervision, data split and compute budget would need separate qualification. This is a local feasibility finding, not a claim that no benchmark exists.

**Adviser-facing thesis package.** The empirical question, four-graph contrast, negative accuracy result, compatible-task bound, attribution control and remaining paper requirement now appear together at the front of the existing TeX. Claim coverage is one trained tabular checkpoint and the explicitly constructed analytical examples. We recommend evaluating the thesis on precise attribution, reproducibility and falsifiable limits. Stability under general drift, task-specific recovery and a new optimizer remain open.

**Milestones and falsification.** (1) Graph and scalar/vector derivations, negative checks, matched-state neural diagnostic, and independent score verification are complete. (2) Recovery is a conditional extension. Before considering any new training, define a reciprocal prefix-by-return comparison, test disjoint adaptation/evaluation scheduling and pre/post risk measurement, choose a useful minimum effect and compute cap, and identify a source-compatible independent stream. A valid study would match A,B against C,B at both A and C returns and compare with fresh, matched-counter-cleared, no-adaptation, and step-scaled fresh controls. Fit the scalar on unlabeled adaptation outputs only; score on a separate return set. Learner gets neither task identity to select a policy nor return labels. Equal storage slots (two moment buffers and a counter), history lengths, intervening examples, steps and resets must be documented. Old labels after pretraining go only to the evaluator. Lower return error would have to follow the matching prefix in both directions and beat the matched controls. Shared recency, a single generally better prefix, or a direction difference without accuracy benefit fails. (3) Next cycle may audit the proposed reciprocal contrast for identification and order confounding analytically, with zero training and no new labels. If the comparison cannot distinguish prefix effects under a feasible information budget, retain the scoped thesis and close the extension. No numerical study is registered by this review.
